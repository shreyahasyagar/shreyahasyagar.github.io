Hi, I'm Shreya. I'm a Senior Software Engineer who enjoys working behind the scenes — designing backend systems that are clean, resilient, and genuinely useful.

Over the past few years I've worked on everything from AWS-powered microservices to GPT-driven automation tools. What keeps me going is building things that reduce friction — migrating a legacy system to improve reliability, or automating a repetitive task to free up someone's time. I'm AWS-certified, trained in prompt engineering, and always up for a good conversation about distributed systems, clean code, or underrated APIs.
