01 — About

Engineering that quietly makes things better.

Hi, I'm Shreya — a Senior Software Engineer who enjoys working behind the scenes, designing backend systems that are clean, resilient, and genuinely useful.

I've spent the last few years building event-driven, serverless platforms on AWS and applying AI where it actually earns its place — deterministic LLM pipelines, retrieval, agents, and evaluation harnesses, not hype. What keeps me going is removing friction: re-architecting a fragile system into a reliable one, or automating a painful manual task so a whole team gets their time back.

I care about the hard parts — race-safe concurrency, cost-aware inference, secret rotation, and the retry / DLQ / rate-governor tooling that keeps distributed systems alive under load. I've worked in a remote-first environment for years, so owning work async across time zones is second nature. AWS-certified, trained in prompt engineering, and always up for a good conversation about distributed systems and clean code.
